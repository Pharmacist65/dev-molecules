# Dev Molecules V2.1 — Release Blocker and Product Integrity Sprint

Status: implementation, local acceptance gates, and evidence present; CI deployment and anonymous-live status are evaluated per published commit

Dev Molecules is a public alpha. V2.1 does not add a new scientific source,
AI narrator, docking workflow, quantum view, or server-side project store. It
closes the gap between the 1,552-record structure index and the product flows
that open those records.

## Active release blockers

1. Every resolved catalog identity opens a stable Basic Molecular Record with
   real identity, structure, provenance, conservative properties, and explicit
   coverage. A missing curated Dossier is not an error page.
2. Curated Drug Dossiers keep deep content separate from Basic Records and
   compress unavailable pharmacology, ADME, synthesis, nomenclature, and
   comparison layers into honest, non-repetitive coverage states.
3. The Home featured molecule is a stable presentation object: atom picking is
   disabled, camera fitting is event-bounded, its identity footer is protected,
   and idle/projected geometry telemetry proves that it neither jitters nor
   clips.
4. Atom selection remains available only in a suitable inspector surface and
   cannot trigger a camera refit or cover molecule identity and controls.
5. Home and Student Spatial copy prioritizes the learner task. Detailed source
   scope remains available on demand; raw review and algorithm terminology
   remains Reviewer-only.
6. TR/EN, typography, Ketcher regression, full-index routing, non-seed records,
   motion stability, empty states, production screenshots, and a ten-second
   Home recording are release gates.

V2.1 is not complete until the documented type, lint, build, catalog, Node,
Playwright, audit, diff, deployment, and anonymous live checks pass against the
same commit.

## Ordered backlog after V2.1

The following work is deliberately blocked on this sprint and must be taken in
order rather than mixed into V2.1:

1. Curate real pharmacology and ADME coverage for 12–20 flagship drugs, with
   direct sources, conditions, units, review status, and correction paths.
2. Add reviewed target–mechanism–metabolite relationships without promoting
   analog, computed, educational, or missing evidence.
3. Expand and independently review Synthesis Atlas and Nomenclature Academy
   depth, including identity-preserving 2D/3D continuity and misconceptions.
4. Only then add evidence-grounded AI/RAG narration with typed outputs,
   citation binding, fail-closed validation, cost/latency measurement, and no
   ability to change scientific review status.

Universal Lookup, a unified Lens, Property Atlas, Expert measurement/QC,
Export Studio, Quantum View, Target & Complex View, private collaboration, and
additional modalities remain later roadmap work. Contracts or placeholders do
not count as shipped product flows.
