## Executive conclusion

No exact-name conflict for **MOLEVREN** or the reviewed MOLEVREN-formative
variants was found on the public web, GitHub, Apple App Store, Google Play,
USPTO Trademark Search, or the EUIPO-coordinated TMview exact-name query checked
on 2026-08-25.

That exact-name result does **not** make the name legally clear.

A material confusing-similarity cluster exists:

1. **MoleVerse — Interactive 3D Chemistry Lab** is a current Apple App Store
   Education product. Its listing describes interactive 3D molecules, molecular
   assembly, synthesis routes, chemistry learning, and scientific data.
2. Current **MolVerse** web presences operate in structural biology, molecular
   discovery, and drug discovery.
3. TMview contains **MOLEV** and other MOLEV-formative marks, including a
   registered Philippine pharmaceutical mark in Nice Class 5 and an expired
   Turkish pharmaceutical record.
4. USPTO contains live **MOLEVE** and **MOLEVY** marks, although in presently
   unrelated goods classes.

The similarity assessment is an inference, not a legal conclusion: MOLEVREN,
MoleVerse, and MolVerse share the prominent `MOLEV-/MolV-` opening, closely
related molecule/universe or molecule/verse concepts, and overlapping
chemistry, education, molecular-visualisation, or drug-discovery territory.

**Preliminary overall risk: MEDIUM-HIGH.**

Until a qualified trademark professional completes word, phonetic, conceptual,
and goods/services similarity searches in the intended jurisdictions and
classes:

- keep `dev-molecules` as the repository and technical-platform name;
- do not purchase a domain or make irreversible metadata changes;
- implement the identity as **“Molevren — working brand”** behind a reversible
  feature flag;
- do not use a registered-trademark symbol;
- do not represent this report as trademark availability or registrability
  clearance.
