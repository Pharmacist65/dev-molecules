# Dev Molecules 2.0 inspiration matrix

Research date: 2026-08-23

The references below are principles, not visual templates. Dev Molecules keeps its own pharmaceutical identity, scientific color conventions, accessibility requirements, and information architecture.

| Reference | Useful principle | Dev Molecules application | Anti-pattern to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Ocean School — NFB](https://www.nfb.ca/education/oceanschool/) | Immersive media is paired with inquiry, activities, and explicit learning outcomes. Content can be approached as a guided journey or as modular resources. | Connect every spatial or molecular interaction to a visible prompt, chapter, worked example, or next lesson. Keep Academy routes modular and dossier sections linkable. | Cinematic media that asks the learner to watch without acting; interaction without a learning objective. |
| [The Sea We Breathe](https://vrbluemarine.com/) and its [education context](https://www.bluemarinefoundation.com/education-resources/the-sea-we-breathe/) | A scientific object can carry the narrative while journey markers explain what to do next. The immersive scene and the educational sequence share one visual language. | Let a featured molecule anchor Home and Story Mode, then transition it into structure, target, ADME, and synthesis representations. Keep a visible chapter rail and reduced-motion equivalent. | Mandatory loading theatre, continuous cinematic waits, sound-dependent instruction, or decorative 3D that hides the learning task. |
| [Aesop](https://www.aesop.com/) | Calm hierarchy, material warmth, deliberate type, and expert guidance can feel premium without looking like enterprise software. | Use paper/lake surfaces, restrained navy type, one dominant action, generous editorial rhythm, and short expert explanations. | Copying retail video carousels, placing every idea in a product card, or turning warmth into low-contrast beige-on-beige UI. |
| [Stripe Press](https://press.stripe.com/) | Dense ideas benefit from an editorial grid, strong reading rhythm, and tactile hero objects. Long-form material can remain inviting when the object and text reinforce one another. | Treat the molecule as a valuable scientific object; use an editorial grid for dossier prose, figures, tables, and source notes. | A WebGL book-like loader, excessive empty theatre, or interaction that delays access to reference content. |
| [Linear](https://linear.app/) | A small number of clear navigation choices, one dominant task, precise interaction states, and consistent density reduce cognitive noise. | Use Home / Drug Atlas / Academy / Lab as the primary IA, global search as the dominant utility, and predictable focus/keyboard behavior throughout. | Generic dark SaaS styling, tiny grey labels, AI-product slogans, or dashboard-card accumulation. |
| [Active Theory](https://v5.activetheory.net/) | Camera movement, spatial depth, and controlled transitions can make navigation feel physical and memorable. | Reuse camera fly-to for family and molecule selection; use 3D-to-2D and bond-line transitions only when they communicate a scientific state change. | An experimental portfolio shell, permanent loader, novelty navigation, or motion that becomes the task. |
| [Unseen Studio](https://unseen.co/projects/) | Restrained compositions can be paired with bold motion and distinctive type when the interaction remains legible. | Reserve expressive motion for selected molecules, reaction arrows, and section transitions; keep core controls conventional. | Cryptic navigation, sound-first interaction, cursor tricks, fragmented type, or visual effects that compete with chemical structures. |
| [Integrated Biosciences](https://integratedbio.com/) | Contemporary biotech communication can be confident, spacious, and specific about the scientific platform without using sci-fi ornament. | Use a clear pharmaceutical proposition, large but bounded headings, controlled black/navy scientific stages, and concise explanation of chemistry-to-biology relationships. | Oversized headlines that push the product below the fold, vague AI claims, monochrome biotech sameness, or treating research claims as decoration. |

## Combined design rule

Dev Molecules should feel like an academic publication, an interactive science museum, and a professional pharmaceutical reference at once. Editorial reading and direct manipulation are primary; cinematic treatment is supporting evidence, never a gate.
